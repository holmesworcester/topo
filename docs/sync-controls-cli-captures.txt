Sync Controls CLI Session Captures
===================================

1. Trigger round for specific peer

    $ topo sync round peer abc123
    peer: abc123deadbeef...
    observed_ids: 3
      aabbccdd...
      11223344...
      55667788...

2. Trigger round for all peers

    $ topo sync round all
    peer: abc123deadbeef...
    observed_ids: 2
      aabbccdd...
      11223344...
